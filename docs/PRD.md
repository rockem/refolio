# Refolio - PRD

## Executive Summary

Paper Marker is a digital reference system designed for analog-first users who manage their information through
notebooks and offline documentation. The product enables users to save pieces of information in a digital system,
receive unique reference IDs, and cite these IDs in their physical notebooks for seamless cross-referencing between
digital and analog workflows.

## Problem Statement

Users who rely on notebooks and analog systems for information management face a challenge: they want to reference
digital information in their physical notes without cluttering their notebooks with lengthy details. Currently, they
either:

- Write full information in their notebooks (reducing space efficiency)
- Manually create their own reference systems (inconsistent and error-prone)
- Abandon the practice of cross-referencing between systems

## Target Audience

- Notebook users and analog enthusiasts
- Knowledge workers who use bullet journals, Zettelkasten, or similar systems
- Students and researchers who combine digital and physical note-taking
- Professionals managing information across multiple mediums

## Core Features

### 1. Information Capture

- Users can save any piece of information (text, links, images, files)
- Simple, distraction-free interface for quick capture
- Support for rich formatting and attachments

### 2. Automatic ID Generation

- Each saved piece of information receives a unique, memorable reference ID
- IDs should be short, easy to write by hand, and human-readable
- Examples: PM-2024-001, REF-ABC-123, or similar formats

### 3. Reference Management

- Users can view all saved information with their corresponding IDs
- Search and filter functionality to find information by content
- Ability to organize information into categories or collections

### 4. Digital-to-Analog Bridge

- Users write the reference ID in their physical notebooks
- Later, they can look up the ID in Paper Marker to retrieve full details
- Supports the workflow: notebook → ID → digital lookup

### 5. Information Retrieval

- Quick lookup by ID
- Search by content keywords
- Browse by category or date

## Success Metrics

- Number of information pieces saved
- Frequency of ID lookups from notebooks
- User retention rate
- Time saved in cross-referencing between systems

## Future Considerations

- Mobile app for on-the-go capture and lookup
- Export functionality for archiving
- Advanced organization with tags and hierarchies
